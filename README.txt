Cameras for AXIS connects your Axis cameras, door stations and recorders directly to Homey, over your own network. No cloud, no recording server and no extra software are needed.

See
- Live video in the Homey app and on dashboards. The quality adapts to how busy Homey is, and a view left open closes itself after a time you choose.
- The camera picture on the device, and a fresh picture with every flow that needs one.

Know what happens
- Motion from the camera's motion detection, Object Analytics and body heat (PIR) sensors.
- People and vehicles (cameras with AXIS Object Analytics): every person, car, truck, bus, bicycle and motorbike is followed and counted once, with colours, bag, direction and how long it stayed. Faces and licence plates as their own events, with the camera's close-up.
- Object Analytics scenarios as alarms, crossline counting with totals per type, and occupancy in an area.
- Motion and visitors per hour, a heatmap of where people and vehicles go, unusual activity and a daily summary in plain words.
- Sounds, day/night, the IR light, inputs and outputs.
- Door stations: call button pressed (with a picture), PIN codes, cards and tags, entry allowed or refused.

Keep an eye on your devices
- Camera problems in plain words: tampering, memory card, network, temperature, power, and someone blocked after wrong passwords.
- Camera temperature, memory card use, power use and PoE power per recorder port.

Control
- Privacy mode: cover the whole picture with one switch or flow.
- Text on the picture, sounds and siren and light, PTZ presets, the IR light, outputs and virtual inputs, and restarting (rebooting) a camera that hangs.

Five dashboard widgets: camera analytics, recent visitors, traffic and activity, heatmap, and an overview of all cameras.

Not every Axis device can do everything. What you get depends on the model, its AXIS OS version and the camera apps installed on it. People and vehicle tracking, boxes, colours, faces and licence plates need AXIS Object Analytics; older cameras (for example AXIS OS 9.80) get motion insights instead. The app asks every device what it supports and only shows that.

Use a separate account on each camera for Homey. Viewer is enough for most things; privacy mode, restart and text on older cameras need an administrator.
